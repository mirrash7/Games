Raccoon Golf

Your arms are the club. Address the ball, swing back for power, swing through to hit. Three island holes: can you beat par?

  • 3 holes
  • Par 12
  • Lagoon RunCoral CarryPalm Parade

What you need

  • A webcam
  • Both hands in view, at your waist
  • ~2 m back, room to swing
  • Chrome or Edge for GPU speed

Checking your graphics card...

The first visit downloads the pose model once (72-143 MB); after that it starts from your browser's cache.

Your video never leaves your device: the pose model runs in this tab.

How to play

  1. 01

    Hands together, low. Hold them still at your waist until READY.

  2. 02

    Swing back for power. Head height is 100%; higher overswings.

  3. 03

    Swing through to hit. On the green, rock your hands side to side to putt.
