Snack Attack

Your palm is a hungry raccoon. Swipe through the snacks, dodge the trash.

What you need

  • A webcam
  • ~2 m of space
  • Chrome or Edge for GPU speed

Checking your graphics card...

The first visit downloads the pose model once (72-143 MB); after that it starts from your browser's cache.

Your video never leaves your device: the pose model runs in this tab.

How to play

  1. 01

    Stand back so your whole upper body is in view.

  2. 02

    Swipe fast with your right hand: your palm is the raccoon.

  3. 03

    Avoid the trash bags. Let 3 snacks drop and the round is over.
