Raccoon Dance

The raccoon coach dances; you copy it like a mirror and hit every pose on the beat.

How to play

  1. 01

    Stand back so your arms fit in view, even raised.

  2. 02

    Copy the coach like a mirror, as if it were your reflection.

  3. 03

    Hit the poses on the beat. Gold moves count double.
