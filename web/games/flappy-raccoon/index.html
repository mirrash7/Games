Flappy Raccoon

Your arms are the wings. Flap to keep the raccoon in the air and through the pipes.

What you need

  • A webcam
  • ~2 m of space
  • Chrome or Edge for GPU speed

Checking your graphics card...

The first visit downloads the pose model once (72-143 MB); after that it starts from your browser's cache.

Your video never leaves your device: the pose model runs in this tab.

How to play

  1. 01

    Stand back so both arms stay in view, even raised.

  2. 02

    Beat your arms down like wings: each flap is one hop.

  3. 03

    Fly through the gaps. Every pipe you pass scores a point.
