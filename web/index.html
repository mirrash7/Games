MOTION ARCADE

Arcade games you play with your body. A pose model watches your webcam and runs entirely in this browser: no video leaves your computer.

  • Snack Attack your hand is a raccoon. Swipe through the snacks and dodge the trash.
  • Flappy Raccoon flap your arms to fly through the pipes.
