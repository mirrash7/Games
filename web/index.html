[02] How it works

Your camera never leaves the page.

The model downloads once (72-143 MB, then cached) and runs on your graphics card with WebGPU, or on the CPU when there's no WebGPU.

  1. 01

    Camera

    Your webcam feed stays in this browser tab. Frames go to the model and nowhere else.

  2. 02

    RF-DETR, in your browser

    Roboflow's real-time detection model runs on WebGPU when your browser has it. Today's experiences use its keypoint model: 17 body points per person.

  3. 03

    What it sees, you control

    Each experience turns what the model sees into something you can do. In the games: hands become cursors, flaps become flight, poses become dance moves, swings become golf shots.
